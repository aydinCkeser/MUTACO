# Paper experiment strategy

Paper-120 B40 MUTACO-versus-NPE is complete and is the flagship baseline.
Without new simulation calls, the retained records support response and
parameter error distributions, paired comparisons, recommendations,
representative reconstructions, accounting, convergence summaries, and timing
figures.

The B40 MUTACO, NPE, and SNPE comparison is complete. A zero-call audit of all
120 stored targets found no identity, coordinate-seed, beta, or record errors.
The three-target B100 operational qualification on frozen cohort positions 0,
49, and 99 is complete: all nine method records succeeded at the exact 909-call
cap, with no failed or retry calls.

The unified append-only Paper-v3 runner is mechanically ready for approved
MUTACO, NPE, and SNPE method subsets. It can reuse frozen Paper-120 targets and
the existing target-specific paired assessment family, then consolidate new
method records with compatible evidence without rerunning completed methods.
The observed locked B100 allocations were MUTACO `16 + 84`, one-round NPE
`100`, and target-specific SNPE `[34, 33, 33]`. The paired disorder,
initial-state, and thermal-noise assessment coordinates matched across methods
within every target. These qualification records are reusable if their exact
identities remain compatible with a later population declaration.

MUTACO, NPE, and SNPE form the primary comparison group. Likelihood- and
ratio-based SBI families remain a separate conditional group whose posterior
decision policies require separate scientific approval.

The remaining-117 Paper-120 B100 declaration passed zero-call TRUBA validation.
It excludes the three reusable qualification targets and caps the remaining
append at 35,451 calls. Validation does not authorize population execution;
submission remains a separate owner gate.
Later studies may address dimension, standardization, temperature, forward
precision, equal-wall-time comparisons, amortization, and analytic mechanisms.
Manuscript reconstruction follows converged experiments rather than preceding
them. Every campaign requires a separately approved execution prompt.
