**The architecture and core scientific workflow are qualified for the private development branch.** The accepted CPU selection, both historical trajectories and the original-versus-refactored comparison on the same TRUBA profile passed. Historical worker-level evidence and exact reproduction of several figures remain incomplete.

The scientific source was `7f4ef91e34fefb00fd5f1f8779f1c69641afb424`. This edition applies the revised publication rule to the same runs and adds a review of the differences against the manuscript. The first report, test outcomes, checksums, scientific references and manuscript artifacts retain their original bytes. ## Population check from saved results

The 120-target graph comparison supplies the manuscript's primary evidence. From the stored paired rows, mean held-out response MAE at B40 was 0.02150300 for MUTACO, 0.04150253 for scalar qLogNEI and 0.07929869 for Sobol. At B100 the corresponding means were 0.01961135, 0.02552101 and 0.05905394. MUTACO had lower error than scalar qLogNEI on **99/120** and **84/120** paired targets respectively, as reported in the manuscript. Separately, recomputing all 12 SBI-zoo cohort means (three methods, two metrics, two budgets) from retained rows reproduced the published summary values exactly. These checks use stored scientific results and leave the missing raw-worker tests blocked.
