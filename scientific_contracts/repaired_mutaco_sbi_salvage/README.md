# Repaired MUTACO--SBI comparison salvage design

**Classification:** prospective design, zero-call inventory; no execution is
authorized by this document.

The historical terminal defect is strictly downstream of the final search
evaluation and final GP fit. It changes the MUTACO recommendation but does not
change any acquisition decision or simulator observation. Search reuse is
therefore scientifically admissible target by target, but only after the
complete raw lineage exists and an offline exact-source reconstruction matches
the persisted response-transform, training-data, and final-model identities.
The reconstruction builds the exact historical model and restores the selected
parameter state recorded in the immutable fit provenance. Re-running the
optimizer is not an identity test: its floating-point trajectory may vary by
CPU and linear-algebra runtime even when source, data, seeds, and fit identity
are unchanged. An offline optimizer rerun is prospective diagnostic evidence,
never historical evidence.

The only permitted source delta is the correction from terminal optimizer Git
blob `84fee3d...` to `76569b6...`, applied to each budget's native source tree.
Every other source file remains native to the historical B40 or B100 campaign.
If exact reconstruction fails, that target's MUTACO search must be rerun; the
rule is based only on evidence completeness and identity equality, never on its
scientific outcome.

All NPE and SNPE recommendations remain immutable. Corrected MUTACO
recommendations are new versioned evidence. After all recommendations are
frozen, all three methods are assessed under one new method-independent context
per target and budget. B40 and B100 use distinct assessment seeds. Historical
held-out responses remain historical context and are not mixed into the new
primary endpoint. This costs 720 assessment calls when every MUTACO trajectory
is salvageable.

The recovery gate verified all 120 B100 MUTACO raw trajectories from jobs
`6214971` and `6215336`: all SHA-256 sidecars match, target coverage is complete,
and every record has 100 observations and 85 fit records. Exact-state replay
first passed at cohort positions 0, 49, and 99 at both B40 and B100, then passed
for the remaining 234 target-budget states. Across all 240 cases, the response
transform, standardized observations, final fit configuration, training-data
identity, and materialized final-model identity match exactly. No terminal
recommendation was generated. Population-wide search salvageability is
therefore established, and no MUTACO search reruns are required.

For (n_{40}) and (n_{100}), the counts of MUTACO targets that cannot be
salvaged and therefore require search replay, the maximum new call count is

\[
N_{\rm calls}=720+40n_{40}+100n_{100},
\]

ranging from 720 to 17,520 before replay qualification. The population result
resolves \(n_{40}=n_{100}=0\), so 720 held-out assessment calls remain. The next
gate is zero-call corrected-terminal exact-source preparation and a frozen
recommendation-generation declaration. Recommendation generation, assessment,
and TRUBA submission remain separately unauthorized.

## Corrected-terminal preparation boundary

The corrected-terminal preparation uses each target-budget case's native
historical source commit and archived selected GP parameter state.  The native
worktree remains clean.  At execution time, the bootstrap replaces only
`src/mutaco/recommendation/_continuous.py` in memory with corrected Git blob
`76569b6...`; it verifies the displaced blob, corrected blob, unchanged
physical-MAE policy blob, native commit, and clean worktree before dispatch.
This composite source is the exact historical model implementation plus the
single already-authorized terminal correction, not a refit under current main.

The frozen terminal objective is

\[
\hat\theta_{b,j}=\arg\min_{\theta\in\Theta}
\frac{1}{d_y}\left\lvert
\mu_{b,j}^{\rm physical}(\theta)-y_j^\star
\right\rvert_1,
\]

using float32 posterior means, 1,024 deterministic Sobol raw candidates, ten
projected-Adam restarts, at most 64 updates per restart, learning rate 0.05,
and tolerance \(10^{-7}\).  The historical terminal seed context is reused by
coordinate, independent of ambient RNG.  Generation performs no simulator
call and no GP fit; it restores the already-qualified selected parameter state.

All 240 MUTACO input cases and the immutable B40/B100 NPE and SNPE compact
recommendation sources are hash-bound in the preparation package.  Terminal
attempts are append-only, failures are retained, and semantic recommendation
identities exclude elapsed timings.  No recommendation is generated by the
preparation gate.  A later explicit gate must authorize the 240 zero-call
terminal optimizations, and all 240 must be frozen before the 720-call paired
held-out assessment can be authorized.

## Corrected-terminal archive

The authorized generation completed all 240 target-budget recommendations with
no failed recommendation, retry, simulator call, GP fit, search call, or
held-out assessment.  Each compact recommendation is bound to the complete
append-only attempt hash, raw trajectory hash, exact-state replay identity,
final model identity, native-plus-overlay source identity, named terminal seed,
and physical-MAE score.  The collector independently recomputes semantic
recommendation identities, terminal seeds, parameter bounds, and physical MAE
against the frozen cohort target response.

The observed return-source distribution is descriptive evidence, not a pass
criterion: all 240 recommendations were refined returns, while both `refined`
and the declared `raw_fallback` remain valid policy outcomes.  The compact
archive freezes the recommendations but does not authorize assessment.  The
next gate must prepare the new budget-specific paired held-out context and its
720-call declaration without executing it.

## Paired held-out assessment preparation

The assessment preparation freezes exactly 720 tasks: 120 targets, two
budgets, and the three methods MUTACO, NPE, and SNPE.  For each target (j)
and budget (b), the three methods share one method-independent context

\[
A_{b,j}=C(b,\mathrm{target\_id}_j,0),
\]

so they receive identical quenched disorder, initial-state randomness, and
thermal-noise coordinates while evaluating their distinct frozen parameters.
Budget is part of both the family definition and coordinate.  B40 uses
campaign seed `92021540`; B100 uses `92021600`.  The resulting 240 context
families are unique, with no cross-budget overlap.

Every task is bound to its immutable recommendation source and identity, the
canonical Paper-120 target identity, the float32 CPU simulator configuration,
and one nominal held-out call.  The endpoint is component-mean physical
response MAE against the frozen target response.  Historical held-out
responses are excluded.  Automatic retry is forbidden.  This preparation
executes no simulator call and authorizes neither TRUBA preparation nor
submission; exact-source freeze and scientific execution require separate
gates.

The production worker preserves each three-method target--budget group on one
logical worker. With 120 workers, rank (r) owns Paper-120 target (r) at
both budgets and has an exact six-call cap. Workers persist a start record,
every success or typed failure attempt, and a terminal call ledger. No retry
path exists. Global collection runs even when a worker exits nonzero, so a
worker failure cannot suppress evidence already produced by other ranks. The
declared Barbun layout uses 30 nodes, four workers per node, and five CPU cores
per worker.

## Paired held-out assessment result

TRUBA job `6251303` completed from exact source
`61f26e368f426be95978991caf656609e954158f`.  All 120 workers completed their
six assigned evaluations.  The collector verified exactly 720 unique task
records and an exact call ledger of 720 attempted, 720 successful, zero failed,
and zero retry calls.  The fresh assessment matrix is therefore complete and
may be analyzed without pooling any historical held-out response.

The deterministic cross-experiment analysis recomputes physical response MAE
from the persisted response vectors and canonical Paper-120 targets, verifies
identical assessment contexts across the three methods within every
target--budget group, and applies the shared seeded percentile-bootstrap,
wins/ties/losses, winner-count, and mean-rank procedures separately at B40 and
B100.  Search-error and surrogate-error quantities remain explicitly
unavailable in this assessment-only compact evidence; they are not inferred or
imputed from the held-out endpoint.

Mean component-wise held-out MAE at B40 is `0.0222885864` for repaired MUTACO,
`0.1315199666` for NPE, and `0.1388317254` for SNPE.  MUTACO has lower paired
MAE than NPE on 113 of 120 targets and lower paired MAE than SNPE on 110 of 120
targets.  At B100 the corresponding means are `0.0227363033`, `0.0683827357`,
and `0.0737980602`; MUTACO is lower than NPE on 102 targets and lower than SNPE
on 95 targets.  In all four MUTACO--SBI contrasts, the seeded 95% bootstrap
interval for the paired mean MUTACO-minus-SBI difference lies strictly below
zero.  These are endpoint results under the repaired comparison contract, not
claims about search-time or compute efficiency.
